CONNECTION SETUP

Display to Pi:
- VCC → 3.3V
- GND → GND  
- MISO → GPIO 9
- MOSI → GPIO 10
- SCLK → GPIO 11
- LCD_CS → GPIO 8
- LCD_DC → GPIO 25
- LCD_RST → GPIO 27
- LCD_BL → GPIO 18
- TP_SDA → GPIO 2
- TP_SCL → GPIO 3
- TP_INT → GPIO 4
- TP_RST → GPIO 17
